The sale and purchase fee, and the loan fee, follow the Solicitors’ Remuneration Order 2023, Table A. The minimum is RM500. Above RM7.5 million the fee is shown at 1%.

Discount applies to those two professional fees only.

Stamp duty is charged to the purchaser. For a citizen or permanent resident it is 1% on the first RM100,000, 2% on the next RM400,000, 3% on the next RM500,000, and 4% after that. A foreigner pays 8% on residential property and 4% on other property. A loan agreement is 0.5%. Duty is rounded up to the next ringgit.

SST at 8% is added to legal fees only, and only while the switch is on. Disbursements stay outside SST.
